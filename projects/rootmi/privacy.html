Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Rootmi respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the app and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

When you sign in to Rootmi using a supported authentication provider such as Google, Apple, or Kakao, we may receive basic account information including your unique account identifier, display name, and email address (if provided by the authentication provider).

We may also store content that you create, upload, or share through the app in order to provide synchronization, collaboration, backup, and other core app functionality.

How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide app functionality.
  • Maintain and improve the service.
  • Respond to support requests.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers that help operate the app, such as authentication, hosting, and database services.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

User Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of the personal information associated with your account. You may also request a copy of the information we store about you. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

User Content

Content that you create, upload, or share through Rootmi may be stored and processed to provide synchronization, collaboration, and backup features.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
