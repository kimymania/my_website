RootMi

Simple, quick chord sheets for your band. Made for iPad, with Apple Pencil drawing and easy sharing.

Coming soon to the App Store Support

Requires iPadOS 15.0 or later. English and 한국어.

Everything a band needs, nothing it doesn't

Simple and quick

Type chords straight onto bars with a chord keyboard built for speed. 7ths, slash chords, sus and tensions, or your own notation.

Linked sections

Link every Verse and Chorus, so one edit updates every repeat. Add repeat signs, endings and key, tempo or time-signature changes.

Play in any key

Change the key and every chord follows. Bass players can view just the bass notes.

Draw with Apple Pencil

Write notes, circle a section or mark a cue right on the sheet. Touch works too.

Setlists

Put your songs in order for the gig, with lyrics under the bars and undo for every edit.

Share with your band

Upload songs and setlists, join groups and share with members, or export a PDF or image for friends.

How it works

1

Set up your song

Pick a key, tempo and sections like Verse and Chorus.

2

Add the chords

Tap bars, type chords and add lyrics. Draw notes with your Pencil.

3

Play and share

Change the key, build a setlist and share it with your band.

Works offline

Songs and setlists are stored on your device, ready for rehearsal rooms with no signal. You only need an account to upload songs or join groups. RootMi has no ads and no tracking.
